# Drake Burning Bridges AI

Turn one portrait photo into a personalized Burning Bridges party video.

**Official website: [drakeburningbridgesai.video](https://drakeburningbridgesai.video/)**

## What is this project?

Drake Burning Bridges AI is a web-based photo-to-video tool designed to put you in the main-character role of the Burning Bridges party scene. Upload one portrait photo; the supplied reference guides the gestures, camera movement and surrounding people, without requiring a custom prompt.

The configured output is a **20-second, 720p, landscape 16:9 MP4** with the supplied soundtrack. The website offers examples, photo-preparation guides, Google sign-in, one-time video credit packs, and private video downloads. English and Chinese interfaces are available.

## How to use it

1. Visit [the official website](https://drakeburningbridgesai.video/).
2. Review the examples and photo guidance, then sign in with Google.
3. Upload one portrait, purchase video credits, and submit your generation.
4. Download the completed video from your private history.

See the website for current pricing, availability and service terms. Public examples were supplied for the project and are not evidence of a completed generation through this service.
